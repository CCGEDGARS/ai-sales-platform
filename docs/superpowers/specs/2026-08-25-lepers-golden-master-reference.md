# Lepers Golden Master Reference

The locked Golden Master benchmark is derived from the approved 16-page `RIHARDS_LEPERS_Production_Analysis_and_VO.docx` production document.

Measured and contract-locked traits:
- complete eight-section production package plus final producer recommendation;
- seven-act dramaturgical architecture when source length supports it;
- editor-facing KEEP / TIGHTEN / REMOVE / VERIFY table;
- VO MASTER with timecode, function, final spoken text and delivery/edit note;
- fifth-diner narrator with active point of view, warm intelligence and non-humiliating humour;
- 16.67% VO target, preferred 16.17%–17.17% at 130 Latvian words/minute;
- concise narrator interventions, normally 8–45 words and never above 55;
- five-beat opening teaser structure when the source supports it;
- 30-second promo VO, 15-second promo VO and four social hooks;
- editorial/factual/reputational risk control;
- concrete sound, rhythm, graphics and B-roll notes;
- final production checklist and decisive producer recommendation.

Runtime principle: variation is allowed in episode content, never in the locked production standard. The current transcript remains the factual source of truth and no Golden Master criterion permits invented story beats.
