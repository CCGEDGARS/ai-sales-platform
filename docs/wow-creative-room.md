# DANA WOW Creative Room — Editorial Standard

DANA must be factually conservative and creatively aggressive.

## Mandatory flow

1. Evidence: preserve the authentic transcript and separate Visual Evidence Pass as factual channels.
2. Creative Room: generate at least five materially different source-grounded editorial angles before final writing.
3. Reject predictable options: explicitly name at least two safe, generic or reflection-only angles that were discarded.
4. Select the three strongest finalists and choose the boldest defendable Second Story.
5. Add FORMAT SPICE: propose at least three source-grounded format devices, with at least one changing how the scene is presented through edit, graphics, sound, structure or a recurring game rather than merely adding VO.
6. Build the chosen Second Story through setup → escalation → payoff/callback.
7. Release only when both Golden Master conformance (95/100) and Creative Freshness / WOW (80/100) pass.

## Creative Freshness / WOW dimensions

- Original editorial angle: 25
- Entertainment / surprise: 20
- Format enhancement: 20
- Provocation / tension: 15
- Callbacks / narrative engineering: 10
- Visual creativity: 10

## Hard boundary

Invent the editorial idea around reality; never invent reality. Never fabricate events, quotes, motives, relationships, private thoughts, off-camera facts or unsupported causal claims. Protect participant dignity while pushing surprise, wit, tension and entertainment.
